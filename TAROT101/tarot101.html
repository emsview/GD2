ABOUT

This site is designed as a learning tool for beginner Tarot readers looking to familiarize themselves with the deck and deepen their understanding of card meanings. It features all 78 Tarot cards, along with interactive elements like the Card of the Day with a quiz section to reinforce learning through regular practice. Rather than focusing on memorization, the site encourages users to recognize patterns and gain confidence in interpreting Tarot readings over time.

WHAT IS TAROT?

Tarot is a 78-card system used for reflection, guidance, and interpretation. Each card contains significant imagery, archetypes, and themes that represent different aspects of the human experience. Tarot encourages you to examine situations from new perspectives by interpreting symbols and patterns that appear in a reading. It is both a structured system and a flexible tool for personal insight.
