KING OF SWORDS

ABOUT THE CARD

The King of Wands represents confident leadership and bold creative direction. Embodying ambition and inspiring those around them through action and intent.

SUIT

Belonging to the suit of Wands (fire), this card holds drive, expansion, and leadership energy.

IMAGERY

Seen as a king on a throne, he holds a wand symbolizing life and creativity. The King is adorned with lions and salamanders, signifying strength and drive. His body is positioned towards the left, appearing to reflect on past instances to confidently inform and execute his next move.

UPRIGHT MEANING

Leadership, honour, ambition, drive, confidence.

REVERSED MEANING

Impulsiveness, haste, high expectations, reluctance, loss of direction.
